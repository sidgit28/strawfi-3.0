### What is EBITDA?
EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization. It is commonly used to evaluate a company's operating performance before financing, tax, and certain non-cash expenses.

### What is revenue?
Revenue is the total income a company generates from selling its products or services before expenses are deducted.

### What is profit?
Profit is the amount remaining after a company subtracts its expenses from its revenue.

### What is P/E ratio?
The price-to-earnings ratio compares a company's share price with its earnings per share. Investors commonly use it as one measure of valuation.

### What is a DCF?
Discounted cash flow is a valuation method that estimates the present value of future cash flows using a discount rate.

### What is market capitalization?
Market capitalization is the total market value of a company's outstanding shares.

### What is free cash flow?
Free cash flow is cash generated by a business after accounting for capital expenditures needed to maintain or grow the business.

### What is a bull market?
A bull market is a sustained period in which asset prices generally rise and investor sentiment is positive.

### What is a bear market?
A bear market is a sustained period in which asset prices generally decline and investor sentiment is negative.

### What is diversification?
Diversification means spreading investments across different assets, companies, sectors, or markets to reduce concentration risk.

### What is risk?
Investment risk is the possibility that an investment's actual outcome differs from what was expected, including the possibility of losing capital.

### What is an investment thesis?
An investment thesis is a structured explanation of why an investor believes an investment may perform well or poorly, including supporting evidence, assumptions, and risks.

### What is a financial model?
A financial model is a quantitative representation of a company's financial performance used to analyze scenarios, forecasts, valuation, and investment decisions.

### What is WACC?
Weighted average cost of capital is the blended required rate of return for a company's debt and equity financing.

### What is terminal value?
Terminal value represents the estimated value of a business beyond the explicit forecast period in a valuation model.
